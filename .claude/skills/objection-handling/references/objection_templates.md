# Objection Response Templates (SMB Tone)

Use these as starting points; tailor to the prospect’s stated pain.

## Price
“Totally fair — tight margins make pricing feel high‑stakes. If we can help your team resolve more tickets per agent and cut manual work, would that make the monthly cost feel easier to justify? What’s your current ticket volume and agent capacity?”

## Timing
“I hear you — timing is always tricky. If we can get you set up quickly and show value in the first few weeks, would that change the timing conversation? What’s the earliest realistic go‑live you’d aim for?”

## Already have a help desk / switching cost
“Makes sense — switching is never fun. If the current setup is costing time or creating bottlenecks, fixing that is usually where the value shows up. What’s the biggest pain your team deals with day‑to‑day?”

## Competitor mention
“That’s a solid option. The key is fit — especially around the channels you rely on and how predictable your costs are as volume grows. Which channels and volume are most critical for you right now?”

## Feature gap / uncertainty
“Good question. I don’t want to guess — I’ll confirm the exact behavior and get back to you with a clear answer. What’s the specific workflow you need to support?”

## Budget cap
“Understood. If we can hit your outcomes within that cap, we’ll keep it there. What outcomes matter most so we can prioritize the right plan and add‑ons?”
